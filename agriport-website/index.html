The Challenge

Why Current Solutions Fail Farmers

Key operational challenges faced in Indian agriculture

❌ Internet-Dependent AI Fails

Over 60% of rural farms lack high-speed connectivity needed for cloud-based crop diagnostic tools.

❌ Camera-Only False Alarms

Vision-only detection confuses lighting changes or water droplets with diseases, causing unnecessary pesticide spraying.

❌ Unsafe Water Scheduling

Manual irrigation causes severe water waste and leads to pump dry-run motor burnouts when reservoirs dry up.

Our Solution

Validated Offline Multimodal System

AGRIPORT cross-validates image inference with environmental field sensors.

📡 Fully Offline

No mandatory cloud subscription or continuous mobile data needed for daily operation.

🔬 Multimodal Intelligence

Combines visual crop features with temp, humidity, and soil moisture to eliminate false alerts.

💧 Safe Irrigation

Automatic soil-moisture irrigation control with ultrasonic reservoir monitoring to prevent pump dry-runs.
